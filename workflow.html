Workflow

As a working adult with ADHD this must be the singular most important thing to ensure I am productive at work, but also to ensure I don't spiral, evolved over many years of working, but never complete, these are some of what I find works for me...

📓

reMarkable

E-ink tablet

Paper-like writing without the paper pile-up. Having one physical place to scribble notes, without a backlit screen pulling my attention sideways, makes a real difference to how well I actually focus.

See reMarkable
✨

Claude

AI assistant

My externalised working memory. Dumping half-formed thoughts somewhere that can actually organise them back into something useful has quietly become one of the biggest changes to how I work.

Try Claude
🍅

Pomodoro Timer

Any variant works

Working in short focused bursts with built-in breaks suits ADHD brains well. I use a simple web-based timer — no fancy app needed.

Try Pomofocus
🔕

Focus@Will

Background music for focus

Background music designed to support focus. The science is debated but anecdotally it works for me when I need to get into a flow state.

Try Focus@Will
